Our story

Family owned, Hallam VIC.

Sayed and Sons Machinery is a family business based at Factory 5, 65-67 Hallam South Road, Hallam. We design our machines for Australian soil, heat and site conditions and build them with quality international components, so parts and servicing are handled locally rather than shipped from overseas.

Every machine we hire or sell is backed by our Hallam workshop, a 5 year / 200 hour warranty and three free services. We import our own machines, run our own transport and keep the parts on the shelf, which is how we keep the prices where they are.
