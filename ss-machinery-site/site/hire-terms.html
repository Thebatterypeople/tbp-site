Hire agreement

The terms that apply to every machine hired from S&S Machinery.

1. General terms

By booking a machine from S&S Machinery you agree to the following terms. All hire charges are exclusive of GST and are subject to change without notice. Fuel is provided at the start of the hire period and the machine must be returned full, or fuel is charged at cost at the end of the hire.

2. Payment and deposits

Hire charges are payable in advance unless a trade account has been approved. A security bond of $500 (mini loaders) or $1,000 (skid steer and excavator) is pre-authorised on a credit card at pick-up or delivery and released within 5 business days of the machine being returned in the condition it left.

3. Damage and maintenance

The hirer is responsible for the machine from delivery or pick-up until it is returned, including loss, theft and damage. A 12% damage waiver applies to every hire and limits the hirer's liability for accidental damage to the bond amount, subject to the waiver exclusions. The hirer must not repair, modify or service the machine; report any fault to S&S Machinery immediately and stop using the machine if it is unsafe. Only operators who have completed the S&S handover induction may operate the machine.

4. Delivery and return

Delivery is provided from our Factory 5 yard in Hallam. Delivery within 25 km is $120 each way; between 25 km and 50 km it is $180 each way; pick-up from the yard is free. All machines must be returned to the yard in Hallam, or be ready for collection, at the end of the hire period.

5. Cancellations

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the start of the hire; later cancellations are charged one day's hire.
